◆ getConstellationByEnglishName()

ScmConstellation* scm::ScmSkyCulture::getConstellationByEnglishName ( const QString &  englishName)

Gets a constellation from the sky culture by its English (translated) name.

The comparison is case-insensitive and ignores non-alphanumeric symbols (e.g. apostrophes, punctuation) so that e.g. "Orion's Belt" matches "Orions Belt".

Parameters
englishNameThe English name to match.
