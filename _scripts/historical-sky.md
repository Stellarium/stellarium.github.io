Recreate the night sky for any historical date, time and location: the script sets the observer location, the date and time (UTC), pauses the simulation, and switches on constellation lines, labels, the landscape and the atmosphere. A second script points the view at the catalog star closest to straight up. Runs alongside historical_sky.inc from the same repository; full set, examples and install notes are in the <a href="https://github.com/dominiclifuchun-cell/historical-sky-stellarium">GitHub repository</a>.
